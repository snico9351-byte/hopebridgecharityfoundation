About HopeBridge Foundation

HopeBridge Foundation is a global humanitarian organisation dedicated to restoring dignity and hope to the most vulnerable: orphans, widows and victims of war and conflict.

We focus especially on India, where millions of children grow up without parents and widows struggle alone. We also reach families in Palestine, Haiti and other regions where conflict and poverty have stolen childhood and security.

We believe no child should sleep hungry, no widow should feel abandoned, and no family torn by war should be forgotten. Through cash donations we provide food, shelter, education, medical care and emergency relief — quickly and with compassion.

We cannot do this alone. Every donation, no matter how small, becomes a lifeline. Join us in building bridges of hope.

Who We Help

🧒

Orphans

Children who have lost their parents to poverty, disease or violence. We provide shelter, nutrition, schooling and the love every child deserves — with special focus on India.

👩

Widows

Women left alone after conflict or hardship. We offer livelihood support, counselling and a community that refuses to let them be forgotten.

🕊️

War Survivors

Families torn apart by conflict in Palestine, Haiti and beyond. Emergency relief, medical aid and long-term rebuilding of hope.

🇮🇳

India First

While our heart is global, the majority of our efforts remain rooted in India — the land where the need is greatest and the impact can be deepest.
